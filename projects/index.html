    Rashtriya Swasthya Sangathan

    NGO Donation & Outreach Platform

    This NGO runs blood donation camps, mobile health clinics, old-age home support, and medical aid for underprivileged communities across India — but had no way for donors or volunteers to find them online. We built a full donation and outreach website: volunteer and donor sign-up, a cause-based donation flow, a news and events section, a photo gallery, and their impact numbers, all in one place. People can now find the organization, understand what they do, and give — without the NGO needing a developer on staff.

    Build noteRuns without a developer on staff — the NGO adds causes, events and gallery entries themselves.

    SA Ethics Biotech

    Concept — never adopted

    Concept Build — Certified Medical Device Manufacturer

    SA Ethics Biotech is an ISO 9001:2015 & ISO 13485:2016 certified manufacturer and importer/exporter of medical devices and blood bank equipment, based in Delhi and Kanpur. After delivering the Rashtriya Swasthya Sangathan project, we built this corporate site on spec — unpaid, unsolicited — to show what a site matching their certifications and B2B credibility could look like. It was never adopted: not live on their actual domain, not paid for, not a client engagement. Shown here as design work, honestly labeled, not as a delivered project.
