How It Works

  1. Fill in your business details, the client's billing address, and one or more line items.
  2. Add tax, a discount, or shipping if the invoice needs them — totals update as you type.
  3. Download the invoice as a PDF, or save it so you can reopen and edit it later.
A decision made building this

Invoice history is saved under a versioned key, on purpose — there's no backend to run a migration against, so a future data-shape change just starts a fresh history instead of crashing on old records.

Features

  • Editable invoice with logo upload, line items, tax, discount, and shipping
  • One-click PDF download, styled and ready to email
  • Invoices save to this browser's local storage — no account, no server
  • Reusable defaults for your business details, currency, notes, and terms

Use Cases

  • Freelancers billing clients for project or hourly work

Advantages

  • Nothing to install and no account required to get a PDF

Limitations

  • Saved invoices live in this browser only — they won't sync across devices
  • No built-in payment collection — this generates the document, not the payment link
  • Clearing browser data or local storage will remove saved invoices

FAQ

Is this invoice generator really free?

Yes — creating, downloading, and saving invoices is completely free, with no account or signup required.

Where are my invoices stored?

In your browser's local storage, on your own device. Nothing is uploaded to a server, which also means invoices won't appear if you switch browsers or devices.

Can I add my logo and business details once and reuse them?

Yes — fill in your logo, address, currency, notes, and terms, then click "Save as default." Every new invoice you create will start pre-filled with those details.

Does the PDF look the same as what I see on screen?

Yes — the PDF is generated directly from your invoice data, not a screenshot, so text stays sharp and the layout matches what you filled in.

Can I edit an invoice after saving it?

Yes — open "History," click any saved invoice to load it back into the editor, make your changes, and save again.
