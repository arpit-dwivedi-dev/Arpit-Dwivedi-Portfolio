Working With Us

Every project starts with a discovery call, not a sales pitch — we want to understand what you actually need before talking about cost. From there you get a written, fixed-scope proposal, so the number you agree to is the number you pay.

Arpit Dwivedi, the founder, stays hands-on through the whole build — there's no handoff between an account manager and whoever actually writes the code. If you have a question mid-project, you're talking to the person building it.

We don't disappear after launch. Fixes, small updates, and follow-up work are part of how we work, not a separate retainer you have to negotiate into existence.
